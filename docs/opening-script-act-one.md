# OLD STABLES — Opening Script

**Canonical in-game opening for Act I: First Lights**

## PROLOGUE

### 1. THE LAST CARRIAGE HORSE

**Narration**

The bailiff’s men lead away Valenne’s last carriage horse. Its empty stall still bears the family crest.

### 2. WHAT THE BAILIFF LEFT

**Countess Cécile de Valenne — Countess of Valenne**

He left the harness. It bears our crest; no buyer wants another family’s pride.

### 3. THE ESTATE LEDGER

**Apolline de Valenne — Acting Administrator**

The treasury can support the estate for forty-two more days. After that, we are destitute.

### 4. ONE ASSET REMAINS

**Count Armand de Valenne**

There is still the brewhouse in the Old Stables. It has stood cold for years, but the copper may yet be sound.

### 5. THE OLD BREWERY

**Apolline de Valenne**

The brewery? It needs repairs, fuel, ingredients, labour, and a brewmaster. None of those comes cheaply.

### 6. THE COUNTESS’S RELATIONS

**Countess Cécile de Valenne — Countess of Valenne**

The Three Lanterns will take one trial cask. Saint-Odile will spare us a fresh yeast pitch. I can open those doors. I cannot make the beer.

### 7. THE COUNT’S PROPOSAL

**Count Armand de Valenne**

Put the copper back to work. Beer sold through the village inn could give Valenne an income again.

### 8. APOLLINE’S WARNING

**Apolline de Valenne**

If the first batch fails, we lose money we cannot replace. I will not approve the expense on optimism alone.

### 9. WHY YOU WERE SUMMONED

**Count Armand de Valenne**

You trained as a brewer. Inspect the place and tell us plainly whether it can earn more than it costs.

### 10. CASTLE BREWMASTER

**Count Armand de Valenne**

The brewery is yours to run. In brewery work, its staff answer to you. The estate does not—not yet.

### 11. THE ACCOUNTS

**Apolline de Valenne — Acting Administrator**

Bring me the cost of the first batch before you spend a franc. Name the risk and I may defend it. Hide it and I will stop you.

### 12. THE APPOINTMENT

**Narration**

The Count pushes an iron key across the ledger. Beside it lies your appointment, waiting for a signature.

## FIRST LIGHT — THE OLD STABLES AWAKENING

### 1. FIRST LIGHT

**Narration**

At dawn, the Old Stables stand locked behind rain-swollen oak and rusted iron.

**Player action:** Open the stable doors.

### 2. THE BREWHOUSE

**Narration**

Dust covers the copper. Soot cakes the hearth. Beneath the grime, an old hammered repair still holds.

### 3. THE ESTATE STAFF

**Narration**

Four lanterns cross the yard. On Apolline’s orders, four members of the estate staff report to the Old Stables.

### 4. JULES LAMBERT

**Jules — Cellar Hand and Maintenance**

I’m Jules. I’ll clear the chimney and make the hearth safe. The copper itself is yours to inspect, Brewmaster.

### 5. MAËLLE RENARD

**Maëlle — Packaging and Sales**

The Three Lanterns will take one trial cask. Make it worth serving, and I’ll see it filled and delivered.

### 6. NOOR BENALI

**Noor — Hospitality Cook**

I’ll keep the work crew fed. A cold brewhouse is hard enough without an empty stomach.

### 7. INEZ DE WILDE

**Inez — Estate Quartermaster**

Brewmaster [PLAYER NAME], the estate granary holds eighteen kilos of sound pale malt. Saint-Odile’s morning cart will bring one fresh yeast pitch.

### 8. THE MISSING HOPS

**Inez — Estate Quartermaster**

There are no hops in store. Old vines grow beside the millstream, but every usable cone must be inspected.

### 9. WORK BEGINS

**Narration**

Jules sets his ladder beneath the chimney. Inez checks the tools while Maëlle and Noor carry out spoiled sacks and broken timber.

### 10. THE COPPER BREWHOUSE

**Jules — Cellar Hand and Maintenance**

I’ll see to the chimney. The old copper patch is ugly, but it held before. Inspect it, Brewmaster, then decide what we can trust.

## What this opening establishes for Act I

- Armand grants brewing authority, not control of the Valenne estate.
- Apolline controls financial risk and can become the player’s governing partner rather than a simple obstacle.
- Cécile’s noble relationships create access to customers and supplies but cannot produce success by themselves.
- The black stable key, old copper patch, estate ledger and Three Lanterns relationship begin as continuity objects.
- The brewery opens with one viable batch, one customer and forty-two days—not with an implausibly complete production chain.
- The closing instruction leads directly into inspection, repair, first mash, missing-hop decision, fermentation and the Night of First Lights.

## Rendering continuity

The first eight prologue beats use the private family-council plate: Armand, Apolline and Cécile discuss the estate without the brewer present. The scene cuts to the appointment plate only at **WHY YOU WERE SUMMONED**, when Armand addresses the brewer for the first time.

The appointment image places Cécile beside Apolline, visually separate from the ledger work and holding sealed correspondence from the Three Lanterns and Saint-Odile. The packaging image uses period-correct returnable wooden casks rather than modern stainless-steel kegs.
