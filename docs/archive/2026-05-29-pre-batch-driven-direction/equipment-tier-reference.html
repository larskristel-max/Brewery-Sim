Brewery-Sim Equipment Tier Reference

Visual reference board for the garage brewery equipment progression. Use this alongside garage-loop-implementation-todo.md when implementing equipment, store cards, garage overlays, and gameplay constraints.

These are reference images, not final game assets. They describe the intended equipment feeling: amateur homebrew at Tier 1, serious garage homebrew at Tier 2, and crowded nano-scale garage pressure at Tier 3.

Tier 1 - Amateur Garage Brewing

Start cheap and believable: all-grain brew-in-a-bag, plastic fermenters, bottle wand, and hand capper.

Starter setup

BIAB pot, bucket, hand bottling

  • 20 L enamel stock pot with brew bag.
  • One plastic fermentation bucket at start.
  • Hand capper and bottle wand.
Garage feeling

Cheap, cluttered, still domestic

  • Equipment should not look professional yet.
  • Garage background stays visible.
  • Extra plastic fermenters can be added until space runs out.
UI reference

Store card direction

  • Cards should show physical equipment, not abstract upgrades.
  • Effects should mention capacity, loss, risk, and attention.
  • Keep language simple and player-facing.

Tier 2 - Serious Homebrewer

Better garage equipment, still not a legal professional brewery. The player can choose all-in-one systems or a three-vessel path.

Brewhouse

Three-tier homebrew system

  • Useful reference for a serious garage three-vessel setup.
  • Can improve throughput by overlapping mash and boil steps.
  • Uses more space than all-in-one systems.
Brewhouse

Stainless three-vessel table setup

  • Cleaner and more capable than Tier 1.
  • Still plausible in a large garage.
  • Good visual target for the Tier 2 three-vessel path.
Packaging

Semi-auto filler option

  • Faster than bottle wand and hand capper.
  • Reduces packaging time and some loss.
  • Still small enough for garage gameplay.
Packaging

Small filler stand

  • Alternative visual reference for Tier 2 packaging.
  • Should feel expensive but not industrial.
  • Can pair with better bottle handling and lower case loss.

All-in-one path

Player may buy up to 3 all-in-one systems because they use less space. A single unit should not drastically speed brewday, but it should reduce attention and allow more parallel work when multiple units are owned.

Fermenter path

Tier 2 fermentation should use small stainless conicals or stainless fermenters around 20-50 L. They reduce loss and risk, but still limit batch size.

Tier 3 - Last Garage Step

Powerful but uncomfortable. The garage is nearly at its physical, fiscal, and social limit.

Brewhouse

Compact nano brewhouse

  • Reference for the top garage brewhouse tier.
  • Should trigger space, power, steam, and visibility pressure.
  • Needs matching fermentation capacity to matter.
Brewhouse

Large basket or hoist system

  • Good reference for large all-in-one handling.
  • Should feel awkward in a garage.
  • Can add cleaning and safety pressure.
Brewhouse

Compact three-vessel nano system

  • Higher throughput, higher complexity.
  • More hoses, pumps, cleaning, and maintenance.
  • Visually pushes the garage toward professional scale.
Brewhouse

Large all-in-one system

  • Reference for Braumeister/Brewtools-style large system.
  • Large capacity should be capped by available fermenter capacity.
  • Better ease of use, not unlimited garage growth.

Tier 3 Packaging And Sales Pressure

Packaging can become faster, but it also makes the operation more visible and more likely to run into invoice, traceability, and compliance pressure.

Packaging format

Cans as a visibility marker

  • Cans feel more public and retail-ready than loose bottles.
  • They should raise expectations for labels and compliance.
  • Use as a pressure signal, not a full canning line yet.
Packaging

Advanced small filler

  • Faster bottling and lower loss.
  • Still feasible before a full professional line.
  • Pairs with larger finished-goods inventory.
Packaging

Very small can seamer

  • Useful final garage packaging upgrade.
  • Not a real canning line.
  • Should increase attention from bars, events, and authorities.

Management Sim Reference

Reference for crisis-style management pressure: the player should have responses when the business gets into trouble.

Crisis mechanics

Airline Tycoon-style pressure

  • Use the spirit of crisis management, not the airline theme.
  • When cash, compliance, or reputation goes wrong, give the player concrete recovery actions.
  • Examples: pause risky sales, take a small loan, reduce public orders, spend on compliance, or accept a reputation hit.
